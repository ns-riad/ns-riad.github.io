<p align="justify">
I am Md. Nazmus Shakib, a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the <i>University of Alabama in Huntsville (UAH)</i>, where I also serve as a Graduate Teaching Assistant from 2025. I completed my achelor of Science in Computer Science and Engineering from <i>Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET)</i>, Bangladesh in 2022. After that, I worked as a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the <i>Green University of Bangladesh (GUB)</i>, teaching undergraduate core courses and supervising student research projects.
</p>

<p align="justify">
My research interests include <i>machine learning, deep learning, intelligent transportation systems, and cyber-physical systems</i>. In recent work, I have studied the string stability of the Follower-Stopper controller through simulations in SUMO. Currently, I am exploring multimodal datasets in transportation, aiming to integrate perception, control, and data-driven modeling for safer and more efficient mobility systems. For additional information, please see my <a href="/research/">Research and Publications</a>.
</p>

<p align="justify">
Thank you for visiting my profile, and I look forward to engaging in meaningful academic endeavors and collaborations.
</p>
