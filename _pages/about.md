I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Engineering at the **University of Alabama in Huntsville (UAH)**, where I conduct my doctoral research in the AI, Autonomy, Resilience, Control (AARC) Lab under the supervision of Dr. Rahul Bhadani.

My research focuses on reliable multimodal learning for autonomous and cyber-physical systems. Modern intelligent systems rarely rely on a single stream of data: a vehicle combines camera frames, LiDAR points, radar returns, maps, and motion traces to make decisions. Models trained on such data often perform well under nominal conditions, but their learned representations can degrade without warning when a sensor is missing, corrupted, or operating in conditions unlike those seen during training. In my doctoral work, I study how the structure of multimodal representations changes under missing sensing and distribution shift. I use tools from topological data analysis to learn representations that preserve this structure and to identify predictions that should not be trusted.

My work combines real-world multimodal driving datasets, such as nuScenes and Snowy Scenes, with controlled CARLA simulations in which sensor availability, weather, and traffic conditions can be varied. Related to this direction, I have worked on degradation-aware camera–LiDAR fusion for headway estimation, the string stability of wave-dampening controllers in mixed-autonomy platoons, and data-driven modeling of electric-vehicle car-following behavior.

I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET), Bangladesh, in 2022. My undergraduate thesis on driver-adaptive abnormality detection received the Best Paper Award at ICEEICT 2021, and I received the Dean's Award for three consecutive academic years.

Before starting my Ph.D., I worked as a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Green University of Bangladesh for two and a half years. There I taught more than fifteen theory and lab courses, served as course coordinator for Object-Oriented Programming and Data Mining, and supervised undergraduate capstone projects. At UAH, I continue teaching as a Graduate Teaching Assistant for the Digital Logic Design Lab. I also serve as a peer reviewer for IEEE ITSC, IEEE CDC, and IEEE SoutheastCon, and I am a student member of IEEE and the IEEE Intelligent Transportation Systems Society.
